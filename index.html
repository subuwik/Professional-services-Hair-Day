Welcome to Hair Day

Welcome to Hair Day, your premier boutique hair salon in Madison, Wisconsin. We specialize in edgy, custom coloring techniques and on trend cuts, taking pride in our warm and relaxing "treat yourself" atmosphere. We also offer expert makeup and nail services!

Our Services

We offer everything from classic styling to experimental colors, plus professional makeup and nail care. Note that we only accommodate a small number of coloring appointments each week, so please book far in advance! To stay inspired between visits, check out our makeup and nail tutorials on our website.

About Us

Hair Day was founded by Pria, a certified colorist specializing in custom techniques and her good friend Garry, a talented hairdresser. Together, they built a "treat yourself" sanctuary. Our team features seven experts: two stylists, three colorists, one manicurist and one make-up artist.

Contact Us

Have questions or want to schedule an appointment? We'd love to hear from you! Reach us by phone, email or via the contact form on our contact page. Remember to book your color treatments early!
